What this does that FM9-Edit cannot
  • Judge a preset. SCAN THIS PRESET walks every scene and reports whether a live signal path exists, names the hop that broke it, and flags scenes that are secretly the same sound.
  • Warn before it surprises you. Parameters live on the channel, so changing one moves every scene sharing it. Those scenes light up and say WILL CHANGE before anything is sent.
  • Undo, and A/B. Neither of which the FM9 has. A snapshot is taken automatically before every change.
  • Audition by typing. 331 amps and 2,237 cabs, filtered as you type and stepped with the arrow keys, searchable by what the cab actually is rather than only its name.
